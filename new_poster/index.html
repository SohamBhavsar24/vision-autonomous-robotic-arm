Vision-Guided Autonomous 6-DOF Robotic Arm

Action Chunking with Transformers (ACT), Receding Horizon Ensembling & Edge Intelligence on Raspberry Pi 5
Team Members: Soham Bhavsar, Toshal Kumbhar, Divyansh Dewangan | PRNs: [1032210001, 1032210002, 1032210003]

1. Introduction

Problem, Objectives & Scope
9pt

Bridging imitation learning with real-time embedded robotic manipulation without cloud computation.

  • The Physical Manipulation Challenge: Traditional analytical Inverse Kinematics (IK) relies on rigid, static target coordinates and fails under camera perspective drift, lighting shifts, or unmodeled servo gear compliance. Conversely, standard end-to-end pixel policies suffer from compounding temporal drift.
  • Research Objectives: Design and physically validate a hybrid perception-learning pipeline: OpenCV detects spatial centroids, while edge neural policies (Behavioral Cloning MLP and Action Chunking Transformers) learn smooth trajectory generation.
  • System Scope: 6-DOF articulated manipulator, overhead Logitech C270 tracking at 30 FPS, $25\text{ cm} \times 30\text{ cm}$ tabletop workspace, dual-block sequential sorting, and zero-jerk Cosine S-Curve transitions deployed on a Raspberry Pi 5.

2. Methodology

Architecture, ACT & Phase-IDW
9pt

Decoupled task decomposition with Action Chunking with Transformers (ACT) and receding horizon ensembling.

  • Decoupled Task Decomposition: Eliminates multimodal place variance by dedicating the neural policy strictly to the visual pick sequence (Home $\to$ Align $\to$ Grasp $\to$ Vertical Lift to safe clearance). The drop sequence is handed off to deterministic Cosine S-Curve waypoints to calibrated bins (drop_locations.json).
  • Pure-NumPy ACT Policy Engine: Multi-Head Self-Attention (4 heads, $d_{\text{model}}=64$, $d_{\text{ff}}=128$) predicting action sequences of horizon $H=24$ ($0.8\text{s}$ lookahead at 30Hz). BLAS acceleration via Apple Accelerate cblas_sgemm flattens 3D tensors into contiguous 2D multiplications ($4.9\text{s/epoch}$).
  • Phase-Aligned IDW Blending: Interpolates across $k=3$ nearest pick demonstrations with phase boundary alignment, preserving downward grasp dip apex without synthetic offsets.

3. Implementation

Hardware, Perception & 3D Twin
9pt

Embedded edge hardware synchronized with zero-dependency Three.js digital twin simulation.

  • Hardware Actuation: High-torque servos (DS3218 20kg-cm Base/Shoulder, MG996R Elbow/Wrist, SG90 Gripper) powered by external 5V 10A supply via PCA9685 16-channel 12-bit PWM and Arduino Mega 2560. Binary framing eliminates serial jitter.
  • 3D WebGL Digital Twin: 100% offline Three.js simulation rendering physical CAD STL meshes in real time synchronized to 60 FPS WebSocket joint telemetry. Table penetration prevention geofencing blocks any candidate commands that violate $Y_{\text{floor\_limit}} = 8.0\text{ mm}$.
  • Spatial Workspace Radar: Real-time 2D SVG table projection ($25\text{ cm} \times 30\text{ cm}$) showing World Origin (Tag 2), active Block Tag 0 & Tag 1 pucks, orientation angles, and reachability geofence boundaries.

5. Innovation

Theta Ablation & Descent-Apex Detection
9pt

Resolving real-world kinematic grasp failure through perception ablation and descent synchronization.

  • 1. Symmetric Block Theta Feature Ablation: Square sponge cubes exhibit 4-fold rotational symmetry ($90^\circ$). Optical ArUco $\theta$ jitter introduces spurious wrist roll adjustments that compromise vertical downward plunge. Removing $\theta$ ($[θ_1..θ_5, \text{gripper}, X, Y]$, 8 dims) stabilizes plunge depth and raises physical grasp reliability to $>90\%$.
  • 2. Descent-Apex Grasp Detection: Overcomes an 85/15 open-vs-closed frame class imbalance in demonstration datasets. Rather than failing at unweighted BCE sigmoid thresholds, the runner kinematically detects downward descent apex ($\text{Elbow} \ge 154^\circ$ after $\ge 35$ approach frames) to lock the claw, hold for 30 lift frames, and cleanly hand off to Phase 3.
  • 3. Edge BLAS Apple Accelerate Optimization: Pure NumPy implementation executes multi-head self-attention on low-power ARM CPU without PyTorch or CUDA dependencies.

6. Application & Outcome

Dual-Block Sorting & Edge Relevance
9pt

Proximity-based sequential multi-object sorting and academic journal dissemination.

  • Dual-Block Autonomous Sorting: Concurrent perception of Block 1 (Tag 0) and Block 2 (Tag 1). Proximity-based spatial sweep prioritizes higher-$X$ downstream blocks first, guaranteeing completely collision-free transit to dedicated drop bins without complex obstacle evasion trajectories.
  • Industrial Edge Relevance: Enables automated pick-and-sort in dynamic micro-warehouses and laboratory handling using sub-$150 hardware components running entirely offline on edge microprocessors.
  • Academic Journal Dissemination: Empirical evaluation framework prepared for peer-reviewed journal submission, benchmarking Markovian MLPs against Action Chunking Transformers across progressive human demonstration tiers.

7. Conclusion & Future Scope

Key Achievements & Research Roadmap
9pt

Key Research Achievements:

  • Successfully implemented, calibrated, and physically validated an end-to-end vision-guided 6-DOF autonomous robotic arm using Action Chunking with Transformers (ACT).
  • Eliminated multimodal drop jitter through decoupled task decomposition (neural pick + deterministic Cosine S-Curve waypoints), reducing trajectory sequence length by 38.6%.
  • Overcame the 85/15 demonstration class imbalance via kinematic Descent-Apex Grasp Detection, achieving a verified 93.3% physical pick success rate.
  • Built a 100% offline edge architecture featuring Apple Accelerate BLAS attention, live SVG Workspace Radar, and an interactive 3D WebGL Digital Twin with table geofencing.

Future Research Scope & Roadmap:

  • Multi-Object Color Segmentation: Expanding perception to color and contour tracking (HSV thresholding) for dynamic unstructured bin picking without fiducial markers.
  • Scaling Demonstration Tiers: Gathering 60- and 90-demonstration datasets across varied approach angles to complete the academic journal scaling matrix.
  • Diffusion Policy Integration: Benchmarking ACT against lightweight Diffusion Policies and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models on the edge.
  • Closed-Loop Visual Servoing: Implementing continuous real-time image Jacobian visual feedback during downward plunge execution.
