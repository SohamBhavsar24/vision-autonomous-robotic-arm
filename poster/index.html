Abstract & Motivation

Autonomous pick-and-place manipulation in unstructured environments presents a dilemma: classical Inverse Kinematics (IK) requires brittle geometric hard-coding that fails under real-world target shifts, whereas pure end-to-end Deep Reinforcement Learning demands millions of physical trials that destroy affordable hobbyist servo motors.

The Solution: We implement a hybrid cyber-physical pipeline integrating:

  • Lighting-Invariant OpenCV Perception: ArUco homography extracts spatial coordinates without neural network training overhead.
  • Imitation Learning (Behavior Cloning): Trains a continuous motion policy from 75 human teleoperation demonstrations.
  • WebGL Cyber-Physical Digital Twin: Enforces workspace geofencing and real-time tracking error validation.

Hardware & Kinematics

The manipulator links are 3D-printed from CAD geometries in Robotic_Arm_3D_Model.STEP, powered by an isolated 6V 5Ah SLA battery rail with an emergency hardware kill-switch.

Link / Joint Actuator Dimension / Role
theta1 • Base Yaw MG996R (Ch 0) Turntable Pivot • L1 = 9.5 cm
theta2 • Shoulder MG996R (Ch 2) Upper Arm Pitch • L2 = 12.0 cm
theta3 • Elbow MG996R (Ch 4) Forearm Pitch • L3 = 10.0 cm
theta4 • Wrist Roll MG90S (Ch 6) Continuous Axial Roll
theta5 • Wrist Pitch MG90S (Ch 8) Vertical Claw Pitch
theta6 • Gripper Claw MG90S (Ch 10) Dual-Gear Claw • L4 = 14.0 cm

Control Circuitry: Arduino Uno Rev3 operating as an asynchronous serial bridge (115200 baud, 6-byte packets at 30Hz) driving a PCA9685 16-channel 12-bit PWM generator with a 500ms safety watchdog timer.

End-to-End System Pipeline

The cyber-physical architecture executes a closed-loop sensing, validation, and actuation cycle across seven interconnected stages:

01 Overhead Vision Perception
Hardware Sensor

Logitech C270 camera mounted on a rigid vertical pole streams 30 FPS video frames (640x480) over USB covering the 25cm x 30cm workspace pad.

02 OpenCV Perception Engine
vision_manager.py

Detects 4x4 ArUco markers with sub-pixel corner refinement. Marker ID 2 sets the static World Origin (0,0); Marker ID 0 tracks the target pick cube (u0, v0, theta0).

03 Calibrated Coordinate Mapping
Metric Homography

Converts optical image pixels (u, v) into metric centimeters (X_cm, Y_cm) relative to the arm base, eliminating camera altitude and tilt drift.

04 Behavior Cloning Policy / Teleoperation
PyTorch / Direct Rate

Learned policy predicts 6-DOF joint trajectories from target pose vector, pre-trained on 75 PS5 human demonstrations recorded at 30Hz with EMA filtering.

05-06 Arduino Uno Driver & 6-DOF Manipulator
115200 Baud I2C

Arduino unpacks 6 raw bytes [0..180 deg] to drive PCA9685 PWM registers. The arm executes the pick-and-place task on 4cm sponge cubes without servo stall.

07 WebGL Cyber-Physical Digital Twin
Three.js 60 FPS

Bidirectional synchronization: calculates real-time tracking error, acts as a virtual collision barrier, and validates policy trajectories in shadow simulation.

4

Cyber-Physical Digital Twin

Rather than a cosmetic visualizer, our WebGL Digital Twin serves five mathematical and safety functions:

01
Sim-to-Real Discrepancy & Stall Detection: Continuously computes tracking error between Forward Kinematics and camera vision to catch motor stalls.
02
Virtual Workspace Geofencing: Enforces bounding barriers preventing link collisions with the camera mount or table surface (Y <= 0).
03
Demonstration Quality Filter: Real-time Jacobian conditioning checks automatically reject jerky or singular human teleoperation demonstrations.
04
Camera Extrinsic Drift Monitoring: Tracks fixed Marker 2 coordinates to alert operators when camera mounting poles flex or drift.
05
Policy Shadow Mode: New policy checkpoints execute inside the 3D twin first; only verified collision-free paths stream to physical motors.

Core Engineering Takeaway
Decoupling visual feature extraction (OpenCV) from motor policy generation (Behavior Cloning) reduces training sample requirements by over 99% compared to end-to-end vision models, allowing practical autonomous policy deployment on affordable edge hardware.
