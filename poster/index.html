Problem & Innovation

Core Manipulation Dilemma: Traditional fixed automation fails when target objects shift unpredictably, while complex neural vision models introduce high latency and require heavy GPUs incompatible with standalone microcontrollers.

  • Decoupled Perception: ArUco planar homography extracts physical coordinates in centimeters at 30 FPS without GPU overhead.
  • Autonomous Motion Control: Cosine S-Curve velocity profiling and coordinated joint trajectory synthesis guarantee smooth, zero-jerk positioning without mechanical shock.
  • Digital Twin & Teleoperation: WebGL 3D simulation enforces workspace geofencing and zero-collision safety; PS5 controller enables precise manual teleoperation.

End-to-End System Pipeline

7-Stage Closed-Loop Cyber-Physical Workflow
STAGE 01 Logitech C270 USB • 30 FPS
Overhead Vision Capture & Frame Acquisition
Rigid 50 cm overhead mount acquires 640x480 MJPEG video at 30 FPS with fixed exposure, capturing the full 25x30 cm table workspace without perspective occlusion or illumination flicker.
Drop Camera Feed
STAGE 02 OpenCV 5.0 • Sub-Pixel Tracking
ArUco Marker Perception & Detection
Real-time binary fiducial tracking isolates base origin (Tag 2) and pick block (Tag 0) in < 12 ms; sub-pixel corner refinement ensures sub-millimeter centroid and heading accuracy.
Drop ArUco Tracking
STAGE 03 Planar Matrix H • Perspective Projection
Metric Real-World Homography Mapping
Calibrated 3x3 homography matrix H maps 2D camera pixels [u, v, 1]ᵀ directly to real-world table coordinates [X_w, Y_w, 1]ᵀ in centimeters with < 0.5 mm spatial precision.
STAGE 04 Coordinated Joint Profile • Zero-Jerk
Autonomous Motion Synthesis & Trajectory Generation
Synthesizes smooth joint-space waypoints using Cosine S-Curve velocity profiling, ensuring zero-jerk transitions, eliminating mechanical shock, and avoiding gear backlash.
Drop Motion Trajectory
STAGE 05 115200 Baud • 400kHz Fast I2C
Binary Serial Protocol & Low-Level Driver
Streams compact 6-byte binary angle frames at 30 Hz with 0xFF sync header framing to Arduino Uno, commanding PCA9685 12-bit PWM servos over 400kHz Fast I2C.
STAGE 06 MG996R & MG90S • Dual-Rail Power
6-DOF Manipulator Actuation & Grasp Execution
Multi-axis servo actuation executes approach, descent, clamping, lift, and placement powered by an isolated 6V 5A battery rail with an active 500ms safety watchdog.
Drop Physical Pick Action
STAGE 07 WebGL Three.js • 60 FPS Shadow
Digital Twin Shadow Validation & Safety Geofence
60 FPS Three.js 3D simulation mirrors manipulator poses in Shadow Mode, enforcing virtual workspace geofencing and verifying collision-free trajectories before physical actuation.

Standalone Edge Intelligence

Autonomous Raspberry Pi 5 Deployment: The entire system runs directly on a Raspberry Pi 5 (4GB RAM) as a 100% self-contained cyber-physical robotic cell.

  • On-Device Loop: OpenCV 5.0 vision at 30 FPS; PyTorch BC policy executes in < 5 ms.
  • Zero Cloud Dependency: Completely air-gapped without external network or latency.
  • Dual-Rail Battery Isolation: 6V 5Ah SLA battery for servos; 5V 5A PD rail for Pi 5 with a 500ms safety watchdog auto-home timer.
