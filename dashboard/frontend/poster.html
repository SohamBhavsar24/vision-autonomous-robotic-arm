1 Abstract & Motivation

Autonomous pick-and-place manipulation in unstructured environments presents a dilemma: traditional Inverse Kinematics (IK) requires brittle geometric hard-coding that fails when objects shift, whereas pure end-to-end Deep Reinforcement Learning requires millions of trials that wear down affordable hobbyist servo motors.

Proposed Approach: We present an affordable, hybrid cyber-physical robotic system combining:

  • OpenCV Perception Engine: Decouples optical tracking from neural control, extracting lighting-invariant spatial feature vectors.
  • Imitation Learning (Behavior Cloning): Trains a motion policy directly from human PS5 teleoperation demonstrations.
  • WebGL Digital Twin: Enforces workspace geofencing and real-time tracking error validation.

Control Hardware: Arduino Uno Rev3 operating as an asynchronous binary serial bridge (115200 baud, 6-byte packets at 30Hz) driving a PCA9685 16-channel 12-bit PWM generator with a 500ms hardware watchdog.

3 End-to-End System Pipeline Flowchart

The cyber-physical architecture executes a closed-loop sensing, simulation, and actuation cycle across seven interconnected blocks:

[1] Overhead Vision Perception Hardware Sensor

Logitech C270 camera positioned on a rigid vertical mount captures continuous 30 FPS video frames (640 x 480) overlooking the 25cm x 30cm workspace surface.

|
[2] OpenCV Perception Engine vision_manager.py

Detects 4x4 ArUco markers with sub-pixel corner refinement. Marker ID 2 sets the static World Origin (0, 0); Marker ID 0 tracks the target pick cube (u0, v0, theta0).

|
[3] Calibrated World Coordinate Mapping Metric Homography

Transforms optical image pixels (u, v) into metric centimeters (X_cm, Y_cm) relative to the robot base, eliminating camera altitude and lens distortion sensitivity.

[4] Behavior Cloning Policy / Teleoperation PyTorch / Direct Rate

Learned policy predicts 6-DOF joint trajectory vectors from target pose vector, pre-trained on 75 PS5 human demonstrations recorded at 30 Hz with EMA filtering.

[5-6] Arduino Uno Driver & Physical Arm 115200 Baud Binary I2C

Arduino unpacks 6 raw bytes [0..180 deg] to drive PCA9685 PWM registers. The physical arm moves smoothly to pick up the 4cm sponge cube without servo stalls.

[7] WebGL Cyber-Physical Digital Twin Three.js 60 FPS Engine

Bidirectional synchronization: calculates real-time tracking error, acts as a virtual collision barrier, and validates policy trajectories in shadow simulation.

4 Role of the Cyber-Physical Digital Twin

In our architecture, the Three.js Digital Twin is not a cosmetic animation—it is a functional mathematical guardian with 5 engineering mandates:

01
Sim-to-Real Discrepancy & Stall Detection: Compares theoretical Forward Kinematics end-effector position against observed camera pose to detect motor gear slips.
02
Virtual Workspace Geofencing: Enforces spatial bounding boxes preventing the arm from colliding with the camera pole or impacting the table surface.
03
Demonstration Quality Filter: Real-time Jacobian conditioning checks automatically reject jerky or singular human teleoperation demonstrations.
04
Extrinsic Calibration Monitoring: Tracks fixed Marker 2 coordinates to alert operators when camera mounting poles flex or drift.
05
Policy Shadow Mode: New policy checkpoints execute inside the 3D twin first; only verified collision-free paths stream to physical motors.

Key Engineering Takeaway

Decoupling visual feature extraction (OpenCV) from motor policy generation (Behavior Cloning) reduces training sample requirements by over 99% compared to end-to-end vision models, allowing practical autonomous policy deployment on affordable edge hardware.
